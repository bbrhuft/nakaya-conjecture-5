## Mathematical points resolved in the revision

The main proof uses Riemann–Hurwitz for the single induced involution. Cyclic point stabilizers are used only to justify the two-to-one lifting count and the absence of common fixed points. The former Klein-four computation is unnecessary.

The total supersingular count is obtained from a flat proper tame quotient, base change for invariant rings, the local branch-preserving node calculation, and the normalization exact sequence for the structure sheaf. No regular resolution is invoked.

The general result covers arbitrary N and arbitrary Atkin–Lehner subgroups in the tame range p ≥ 5, p not dividing N. N=1 is treated separately when lifting fixed points. The N=2 exceptional shape is caused by the complementary 2-adic factors in r_p(2p); r_2(2p) itself cancels from the genus calculation.

The computational appendix does not assume the expected count to construct the Hauptmodul or the supersingular polynomial. The fixed-point enumeration is independent of the RHS class-number implementation, but it uses the same classical mathematical classification. This distinction is explicit.
